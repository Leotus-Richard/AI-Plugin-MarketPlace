# VOICE & EDGE Creator

A rigorous writing and creative direction system designed to eliminate "synthetic sameness," AI slop, corporate jargon, and unverified claims through the **VOICE** writing pipeline and the **EDGE** creative framework.

## When to Use

- The user types `/voice` or `/edge`.
- The user wants to write or edit an article, memo, post, script, or pitch that sounds unmistakably human and original.
- The user wants to brainstorm creative angles, visual hooks, or campaign concepts that break away from predictable AI defaults.

## Steps

### 1. `/voice` - The VOICE Anti-Slop Writing Pipeline

Guide the user through five stages to ensure their writing has human fingerprints and intellectual weight:

1. **Verified (Fact & Citation Audit):**
   - Extract every factual claim, statistic, date, name, and quote from the draft or outline.
   - Mark each item `[Verified]`, `[Unverified]`, or `[Disputed]`.
   - Search for primary sources to ground every claim, or flag unverified claims for removal. Never fabricate citations.
2. **Owned (Fingerprint Interview):**
   - **Do not write the draft yet** if personal context is missing.
   - Interview the user **one question at a time** about what they have personally seen, built, believed, learned, or got wrong regarding the topic.
   - Push back whenever an answer is vague or generic. Stop only when you have extracted up to 5 concrete, lived details that belong uniquely to the user.
3. **Insightful (The "So What?" Stress Test):**
   - Help the user complete the 3-sentence differentiation frame:
     1. *"Most people believe [X]."*
     2. *"I believe [Y]."*
     3. *"The difference in practice is [Z]."*
   - Act as an intelligent skeptic: attack the user's thesis by asking *"Who is arguing the opposite?"* and *"So what?"* until a non-obvious angle emerges.
4. **Clear (The 9-Year-Old & Read-Aloud Audit):**
   - Summarize what the user's draft says as if explaining it to a smart 9-year-old.
   - Highlight every sentence where the reader has to guess the meaning, where humor/sarcasm deflects from candor, or where abstract corporate jargon hides a simple truth.
   - Rewrite bloated abstractions into plain, spoken-cadence English.
5. **Engaging (Narrative Tension Check):**
   - Evaluate line-by-line momentum: *"Does this sentence make the reader need the next one?"*
   - Ruthlessly cut throat-clearing introductions, repetitive transitions, and platitudes.

### 2. `/edge` - The EDGE Creative Differentiation Workflow

When brainstorming creative concepts, campaigns, openings, or product positioning:

1. **Differentiated (Escape the Default):**
   - First, explicitly describe **the most obvious, cliché AI default** for this prompt (e.g., "a burnout app opening with a peaceful beach sunset") and throw it in the trash.
   - Generate **5 unconventional angles** that move far away from that default.
   - Select the single angle most likely to make an audience stop in the first 3 seconds and explain why.
2. **Exacting (Ruthless Rejection):**
   - Generate multiple distinct variations of the chosen hook or concept, then violently prune weak options—explaining the taste and craft trade-offs behind the surviving pick.
3. **Grounded (Physical Weight):**
   - Replace weightless abstractions with tactile, specific, real-world details (sensory mechanics, exact numbers, real constraints).
4. **Emotional (Contextual Resonance):**
   - Anchor the piece in a specific human emotional tension (e.g., relief, quiet dread, earned pride, grief, intimacy) rather than generic positivity.

## Gotchas

- Ban empty AI-isms ("In today's fast-paced world," "delve," "testament," "tapestry," or hollow aphorisms like "love without structure is a liability").
- Never skip the **Owned** interview when drafting personal or thought-leadership pieces; AI fluency without user lived experience produces forgettable work.
